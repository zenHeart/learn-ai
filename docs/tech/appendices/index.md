# Appendix Guide

**What this is**: the appendix area holds content that does not belong to the pyramid argument line but still must have a place — training bridges, real-world cases, course notes, and historical methodology archives. They answer "what else is worth reading", not "what to learn next".

**Boundary statement**: **appendices do not participate in the pyramid argument order**. The mainline runs from the [tech map](../) through layers 0→5; appendix pages can be read at any time but are never cited as prerequisites by mainline chapters. Bridge pages (status: bridge) outsource deep theory to [Learn LLM](https://llm.zenheart.site/); case pages (status: case) supply real-world evidence; legacy pages preserve content that the mainline has absorbed or superseded.

## Reading advice

- **Solving a current problem**: go back to the [tech map](../) and follow the symptom-driven decision tree; do not start from appendices.
- **Want model-side theory** (training, alignment, multimodal internals): go straight to [Learn LLM](https://llm.zenheart.site/); this repo keeps only engineering-decision bridges.
- **Want to see how others did it**: enter [case studies](./cases/) and filter by your layer.
- **Tracing the provenance of this repo's content**: course notes and the methodology archive keep links to original sources.
